# ChoreWars

**Defend your Home Base. Together.** | **Verteidigt eure Basis. Gemeinsam.**

[![License: MIT](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-MIT-yellow.svg)](https://opensource.org/licenses/MIT)

ChoreWars is a gamified household task management app for Nextcloud that transforms cleaning into a cooperative tower defense game. Keep your home clean by defending it against "The Dust" - cute but persistent creatures that invade neglected rooms.

## 🎮 Concept

ChoreWars combines practical household task management with engaging game mechanics:

- **Cooperative Base Defense**: Your home is your base. Work together to keep it clean.
- **Visual Motivation**: See rooms get invaded by cute dust monsters when tasks are overdue
- **No Game Over**: Different levels of chaos vs. order - motivate, don't punish
- **Family/Roommate Friendly**: Share tasks, compete on leaderboards, earn team bonuses

## ✨ Key Features

### Task Management (Sweepy-inspired)
- **Rooms & Floors**: Organize your home spatially with rooms grouped into floors or custom collections
- **Flexible Scheduling**: Tasks with customizable frequency (daily, weekly, monthly, etc.)
- **Difficulty Levels**: 1-5 stars (1 = quick task, 5 = major cleaning)
- **Priority System**: 3 levels (urgent/normal/can wait)
- **Multi-Room Tasks**: Assign tasks to multiple rooms (e.g., vacuuming with robot)
- **Smart Scheduling**: Plan tasks based on your availability and preferences

### Gamification
- **The Dust Invasion**: Cute pixel monsters spawn in overdue rooms
- **Satisfying Feedback**: Animations and effects when completing tasks
- **House Health Bar**: Shared progress indicator for the whole household
- **Team Bonuses**: Combo multipliers for coordinated cleaning
- **Weekly Medals**: Recognition for different play styles (Tank, Scout, etc.)

### Collaboration
- **Multi-User Support**: Share your home with family or roommates
- **Leaderboards**: Friendly competition
- **Task Approval**: Parents can review children's completed tasks
- **Shared Progress**: Everyone contributes to the House Health

## 🏗️ Technical Stack

- **Platform**: Nextcloud App
- **Frontend**: Vue.js 3 (Nextcloud standard)
- **Backend**: PHP (Nextcloud App API)
- **Database**: SQLite/MySQL/PostgreSQL (Nextcloud DB abstraction)
- **Graphics**: Pixel art sprites with CSS animations
