# ChoreStrike

**Defend your Home Base. Together.**

[![License: MIT](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-MIT-yellow.svg)](https://opensource.org/licenses/MIT)
[![Codeberg](https://img.shields.io/badge/Codeberg-292929?style=flat&logo=codeberg&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/ChoreStrike/ChoreStrike)

ChoreStrike is a gamified household task management app for Nextcloud that transforms cleaning into a cooperative base defense game! Keep your home clean by defending it against "The Dust" — cute but persistent little creatures that invade neglected rooms.

## 🎮 Why ChoreStrike?

Doing chores doesn't have to be a grind. ChoreStrike combines practical task management with engaging, family-friendly game mechanics.

- **Team Up:** Your home is your base. Work together with your family or roommates to keep it shining.
- **Visual Motivation:** See rooms get invaded by tiny dust monsters when tasks are overdue. 
- **Stay Positive:** No "Game Over"! We believe in motivating, not punishing.
- **Share the Load:** Flexible scheduling, smart energy models, and shared progress tracking.

## ✨ Current Features

- **Smart Organization:** Group your tasks logically by rooms and floors.
- **Flexible Scheduling:** Set daily, weekly, or custom intervals for your chores.
- **The Dust Invasion:** Watch cute pixel monsters spawn when a room needs attention—and enjoy the satisfying feedback of clearing them out!
- **House Health Bar:** A shared progress indicator for the whole household to see how you're doing.
- **Nextcloud Integration:** Seamlessly integrated into your existing Nextcloud environment.
- **Multilingual:** Ready for English and German (more to come!).

## 🏗️ Technical Stack

- **Platform**: Nextcloud App
- **Frontend**: Vue.js 3 (Composition API)
- **Backend**: PHP (Nextcloud App API)
- **Database**: SQLite/MySQL/PostgreSQL
