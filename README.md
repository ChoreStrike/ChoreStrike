# ChoreStrike

**Defend your Home Base. Together.**

[![License: MIT](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-MIT-yellow.svg)](https://opensource.org/licenses/MIT)
[![Codeberg](https://img.shields.io/badge/Codeberg-292929?style=flat&logo=codeberg&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/ChoreStrike/ChoreStrike)

ChoreStrike is a gamified household task management app for Nextcloud that transforms cleaning into a cooperative base defense game. Keep your home clean by defending it against "The Dust" - cute but persistent creatures that invade neglected rooms.

## 🎮 Concept

ChoreStrike combines practical household task management with engaging game mechanics:

- **Cooperative Base Defense**: Your home is your base. Work together to keep it clean.
- **Visual Motivation**: See rooms get invaded by cute dust monsters when tasks are overdue
- **No Game Over**: Different levels of chaos vs. order - motivate, don't punish
- **Family/Roommate Friendly**: Share tasks, compete on leaderboards, earn Team Sparks bonuses and weekly medals

## ✨ Key Features

### Task Management
- **Rooms & Floors**: Organize your home spatially with rooms grouped into floors or custom collections
- **Flexible Scheduling**: Tasks with customizable frequency (daily, weekly, monthly, etc.)
- **Energy Model**: 1-5 energy per task (~5 minutes per energy, approximate)
- **Priority System**: 3 levels (urgent/normal/can wait)
- **Multi-Room Tasks**: Assign tasks to multiple rooms (e.g., vacuuming with robot)
- **Smart Scheduling**: Plan tasks based on availability, presence days, and daily energy budgets
- **Reminder Settings**: Configurable task reminders synchronized with Nextcloud task clients
- **Bidirectional Completion Sync**: Complete tasks in ChoreStrike UI or in synced task clients

### Gamification
- **The Dust Invasion**: Cute pixel monsters spawn in overdue rooms
- **Satisfying Feedback**: Animations and effects when completing tasks
- **House Health Bar**: Shared progress indicator for the whole household
- **Team Sparks Bonuses**: Combo multipliers for coordinated cleaning
- **Weekly Medals**: Recognition for different play styles (Team Anchor, Flow Keeper, etc.)

### Collaboration
- **Multi-User Support**: Share your home with family or roommates
- **Leaderboards**: Friendly competition
- **Task Approval**: Parents can review children's completed tasks
- **Shared Progress**: Everyone contributes to the House Health

## 🏗️ Technical Stack

- **Platform**: Nextcloud App
- **Frontend**: Vue.js 3 (Nextcloud standard)
- **Backend**: PHP (Nextcloud App API)
- **Database**: SQLite/MySQL/PostgreSQL (Nextcloud DB abstraction)
- **Graphics**: Pixel art sprites with CSS animations
