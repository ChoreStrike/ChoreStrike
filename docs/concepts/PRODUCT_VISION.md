# Product Vision

## Mission

ChoreWars turns household maintenance into a cooperative, motivating experience for families and shared homes.

## Design Principles

1. Motivation over punishment.
2. Cooperative progress over individual pressure.
3. Fairness over raw output.
4. Practical utility first, playful layer second.
5. Mobile-first daily usability through Nextcloud ecosystem sync.
6. **Simplicity for all ages**: The interface must be intuitive and accessible for children, seniors, and everyone in between. No complex workflows, minimal steps to complete tasks, clear visual feedback.

## Core Product Outcomes

- Households can define recurring chores that are actually completed.
- Every member gets a fair share based on time budget and availability.
- Completion can happen from ChoreWars UI or any connected task client.
- Progress stays visible and encouraging without military language.

## Non-Goals

- No punitive mechanics or hard failure states.
- No dependency on external SaaS services.
- No mandatory competitive gameplay.

## Terminology Baseline

- Use "energy" as the effort unit.
- Avoid military resource terms for rewards (for example, avoid "ammo").
- Use "Team Sparks" as the default reward currency name.

## Locked Decisions (MVP)

- Reward currency name: Team Sparks.
- Parent/caregiver balancing model: Keep contribution score and Team Sparks separate, with no role multiplier in MVP.

## Open Decisions

- Whether cooperative goals are per week or per month.
